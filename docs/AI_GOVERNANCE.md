# AI Governance

- AI recommends; backend services execute.
- Provider, model version, confidence and fallback status are stored with complaint analysis.
- Safety risks require human review.
- Technician recommendations cannot assign technicians.
- Chatbot intents are restricted per role and sensitive actions require confirmation.
- Knowledge answers include source references and safety warnings.
- AI analysis, recommendations, predictions, incidents, queries, chatbot decisions and imports create audit events.
- Residents remain scoped to personal records, Block Admins to their block, Technicians to assigned work, and Security to visitor operations.

Fallback rules are deliberately conservative for electrical water contact, gas, fire, lift emergencies, and structural damage.
