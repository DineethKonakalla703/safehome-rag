# Project Overview

## Problem statement

Residential communities often handle complaints through calls, chat messages, paper records, and disconnected spreadsheets. Residents cannot reliably track progress, block administrators lack a focused operational view, and maintenance or billing updates are difficult to audit.

## Proposed system

SafeHome-RAG brings the essential service workflow into one interface: a resident records a complaint, deterministic rules highlight safety concerns, an authorized administrator assigns a technician and updates progress, and a billing record completes the service trail.

## Novelty as integration

The project’s novelty is the integration of resident reporting, explainable risk classification, scoped administration, maintenance coordination, activity history, and billing. Priority 1 proves that end-to-end interaction before later infrastructure or AI work is introduced.
